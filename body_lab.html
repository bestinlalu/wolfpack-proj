Body Lab

A personal scientist for your body. When your body reacts differently to a familiar situation, the agent investigates quietly, opens a hypothesis, and confirms it from your everyday life. No journaling, and no "something was unusual" alerts: you only hear from it when it has an answer.

  • 01Today: solved cases and confirmed discoveries, each leading with the why, plus rank, an auto-tracked quest and the four labs.
  • 02Case: two similar meals, two different spikes. Data checks first, then what was different.
  • 03Discoveries: patterns confirmed by repeated natural experiments become cards.
  • 04Notebook: open hypotheses, evidence so far, and how many surprises turned out to be noise.
